The basics

What the place is, and whether you are renting it out or selling it.

3

Price and bills

The figure clients compare you on, and everything they will owe on top of it. Both belong here, because a rent with three hidden bills behind it is not the rent.

Leave this blank if you price each room separately below. The cheapest room becomes the "from" price.
How many months the tenant must pay at once for this room, shop or office. Ignored for properties listed for sale.
The commission the tenant pays you for the deal, shown separately from the rent. Leave it blank to use the standard one month's rent, or type any amount.

Additional costs, the bills

Bills the tenant pays on top of the rent: electricity, water, garbage, security, internet and anything else. Add each one so clients see the full monthly cost before they call. Leave the amount blank for "metered", meaning pay as you use, or for "included".

4

Rooms, categories and prices

List every kind of room or space in this property and what each one costs: singles, a master, a shop frame, a godown, a parking bay, anything. Tap a suggestion to add it, or type your own name for it. The cheapest one becomes the "from" price clients see.

The property as a whole

Only if the whole property is the thing being let or sold. A plot you are letting room by room can leave these at zero.
